Answering assessor questions

“Tell us about a session that didn’t work.” A four-part shape that keeps answers under two minutes and full of evidence.

Module 8Coaching License Lab
1

Context

Where and when, in one or two sentences.

“U17s, week 7. The session was the counter-press after we’d regained only 15% of losses within five seconds against Westside.”

›
2

Decision

What you chose, and why.

“I used a 6v6+3 game with a point for regains inside five seconds, because I wanted the moment of loss to happen often.”

›
3

Evidence

How you know what happened.

“The video showed 2.8 stoppages every ten minutes and only 12% questions. Intensity dropped every time I stopped play.”

›
4

Change

What you’d do differently.

“I now keep stoppages to one per practice, coach in flow, and ask one question when I do stop. Next match: 24% regains.”

For a former international: use the playing career as a resource, then show how you coach players who don’t think like you did. Assessors listen for that balance.

Watch for
Opinions without evidence. Name the number, the clip or the player response.
No change at the end. Reflection means saying what you’d do differently.
Overlong answers. Rehearse out loud to two minutes, recorded.
