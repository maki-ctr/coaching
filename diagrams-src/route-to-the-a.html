Pro License

Later

A-Youth

Focus: developing players for the professional game, in a youth performance environment (U13–U19).

Needs: B held at least a year, and access to an academy-type environment.

A-Senior

Focus: leading a senior team in a high-performance environment.

Needs: B held at least a year (or a confederation A), and a senior team in a U.S.-based competition, or a college team.

Freddy’s decision

Pick the track by the team he can coach for the whole A course, not by preference. Default in the app: A-Senior.

Watch for
The waiver isn’t automatic. The hosting member decides, and the criteria can change. Confirm them before applying.
The 12 months count from the B being awarded, not from the start of the course.
No team, no course. Both the B and the A need a qualifying team for the full course.
