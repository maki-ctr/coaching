Player development plans

A four-corner plan for Marcus, Harbor FC’s central midfielder (16), plus the growth and maturation facts that shape what’s fair to expect.

Marcus, central midfielder, 16
Review every 2 weeks on video

Technical

Strength: Clean first touch under pressure.

Target: Receive on the half-turn more often.

Tactical

Strength: Reads danger well.

Target: Scan twice before receiving in midfield.

Physical

Strength: Good endurance.

Target: Improve repeated-sprint ability.

Psychosocial

Strength: Quiet in the group.

Target: Organise the players around him.

Built into team training: a scanning cue in every possession practice, a clip review every two weeks, and captaining one small-sided game a week.

Growth and maturation

Peak height velocity

The fastest point of the growth spurt: around 13.5–14 for boys and around 11.5–12 for girls, with wide variation.

Relative age effect

Players born early in the selection year are over-selected, often because they’re more mature, not more talented.

Bio-banding

Grouping by maturity status rather than birth year, so early and late developers get the right challenge.

Growth-related pain

Knee or heel pain around the spurt is common. Manage load and refer for assessment.

Watch for
Vague targets like “get better at passing”. Make them specific, timed and checkable.
Judging late maturers on current physical output instead of decisions and technique.
Targets set for the player, not with them. Agree them in a one-to-one.
