Flags the app raised

  • Lots of stoppages for the time: could some be in-flow cues?
  • More general praise than specific feedback: name the cue or the decision.
  • Few questions: try one guided-discovery question at each stoppage.
  • Instruction-heavy: mix in questions so players own the solution.

Prompts for reflection, not rules. Judge each one against the session topic.

Watch for
Treating the flags as targets. A good stoppage beats three vague in-flow shouts.
“Good job” as feedback. It feels supportive but tells the player nothing to repeat.
Counting from memory. Log from video; coaches underestimate how much they talk.
