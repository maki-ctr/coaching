The Harbor FC game model

How the U17s want to play in every moment. It stays the same all season; each game plan adapts it to one opponent.

Module 2Coaching License Lab
Base shape 4-3-3, attacking left to right.
Identity

Brave on the ball, aggressive without it, ruthless in transition. Players who make decisions, not players who wait for instructions.

In possession

Build with a plus-one. Find the free player between the lines. Attract to release. Arrive in the box with numbers.

Out of possession

Compact and narrow. Press on triggers. Defend forward.

Transition to defend

Five-second counter-press. Recover centrally if the press is broken.

Transition to attack

First look forward. Secure, then rebuild.

Set pieces

Defending corners: mixed. Attacking corners: two routines.

Watch for
Principles you can’t train or measure. If you can’t design a practice or a KPI for it, rewrite it.
Too many principles. Three or four per moment is plenty; players can’t act on twenty.
Confusing model and plan. The plan changes each week. The model changes only after a season’s evidence.
