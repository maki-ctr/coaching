Half-time in three minutes
0:00–1:00
Settle
Water, breathing. Let the emotion drop. Staff confer; players talk to each other.
Staff agree the two points while players recover.
1:00–2:30
Two or three points
Specific, linked to the game plan, aimed at the units they affect.
“Front three: press their left centre-back on the backward pass. Midfield: the first two seconds after we lose it, attack the ball.”
2:30–3:00
One message
The single thing every player takes back out.
“Win it back in five seconds.”
