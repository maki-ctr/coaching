## Leadership

- Describe your coaching philosophy in under a minute. What would an observer see on the field that proves it?
- How has your playing career shaped how you coach, and where could it limit you?
- Tell us about a conflict with a player or staff member. How did you handle it, and what would you do differently?
- How do you build an environment where players own their development?
- How do you get feedback on your own coaching? What did you change because of it?

## Coaching training

- Walk us through last week's training. How did each session link to your game model?
- Tell us about a session that didn't work. What did you see, and what did you change?
- How do you decide when to stop play and when to coach in flow?
- How do you challenge your best player and your weakest player in the same practice?
- How do you manage training load across a week with one game?

## Coaching games

- Explain your game model in two minutes. Which principle matters most, and why?
- Talk us through your plan for your last game. What changed at half-time, and why?
- You're 2–0 down at half-time. What do you say in the first minute?
- How do you use substitutions, and what makes you change shape?
- How do you prepare players for an opponent without overloading them?

## Player development

- Present one player's development plan. How will you know it's working?
- How do you account for growth and maturation in selection and training?
- A player is talented but not trusted by teammates. What's your plan?
- How do you balance team results with individual development?
- Former professionals sometimes coach the player they were. How do you avoid that?

## Performance analysis

- Which three KPIs best reflect your game model, and why those?
- Show us how analysis from a match changed your next training session.
- How do you present analysis to players so they act on it?
- When do you ignore the data?
- How do you evaluate your own coaching across a season?
